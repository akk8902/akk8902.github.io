I am a PhD researcher in the Satellite Geodesy Laboratory at the
Interdisciplinary Centre for Water Research (ICWaR), Indian Institute of
Science (IISc), Bengaluru. My supervisor is Dr. (Ing.) Bramha D. Vishwakarma.

Sea level does not change evenly across the globe, and my research asks why.
I study how mass lost from ice sheets and glaciers, together with changes in
terrestrial water storage, leaves its imprint on regional sea level through
sea level fingerprints. A central thread of my work is connecting ice dynamics
to these fingerprints, so that where and how fast ice changes can be traced
into the spatial variability of regional sea level, and into closing the sea
level budget.

I came to geodesy from environmental science and field ecophysiology, so I
bring observational experience alongside satellite geodesy, hydrological
modelling and machine learning.

## Research interests

- Sea level fingerprints and drivers of sea level variability
- Sea level budget closure
- Ice sheet and sea level system modelling
- Terrestrial water storage reconstruction using machine learning
- Hydrological modelling of runoff and soil moisture
