Five-week course by Prof. Sankar Arumugam (NC State University) under the US-India Educational Foundation. It covered the global water and energy balance, climate variability and change (ENSO, IOD, MJO, CMIP6), seasonal-to-climate forecasting, and downscaling and data assimilation, including Kalman and Ensemble Kalman filtering.
