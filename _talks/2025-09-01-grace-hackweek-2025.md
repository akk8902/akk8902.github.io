Online school on satellite gravimetry and time-variable gravity, with lectures and hands-on labs using real GRACE and GRACE-FO data. Topics ranged from the basics of geodesy and orbit determination to gravity field recovery, signal processing on the sphere, and hydrological, oceanic and cryospheric applications.
