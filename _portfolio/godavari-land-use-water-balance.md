Course project for Watershed Modelling at IISc (Aug to Dec 2025). I assessed how land use/land cover (LULC) change alters monthly runoff and soil moisture, combining the process-based SCS Curve Number model with the spatially lumped abcd hydrologic model.

Inputs: GLEAM potential evapotranspiration and soil moisture, CWC streamflow, IMD rainfall, ESRI land cover, FAO-HWSD soil texture and HYSOGs hydrologic soil groups.
