An independent machine learning project (Aug 2026). I reconstructed terrestrial water storage anomalies (TWSA) across the global land area, excluding glaciers and ice sheets, by benchmarking LSTM, GRU, BCNN, Transformer and harmonic regression models.

The domain was divided into Eurasia, Africa, North America, South America, Australia and the remaining regions.
