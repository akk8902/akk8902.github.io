AgriSense is a mobile app I developed part-time (Aug to Sep 2026). It turns open weather and satellite data into field-level early warnings and irrigation guidance for farmers.

- **Data:** Open-Meteo forecasts, NASA POWER data for FAO-56 Penman-Monteith evapotranspiration, and Sentinel-2 vegetation indices.
- **Models:** degree-day models for crop growth stage and pest emergence.
- **Advisories:** an AI assistant provides daily advisories.

Public release on app platforms is planned.
